Where visits came from

Each match

A visit that landed on a matching page from a matching campaign shows on both rows, so rows can add up to more than the total. The total counts it once.

More detail

Visits count people who landed on the page from outside the site, so a page people reach by clicking around shows few visits. Downloads are form fills on a report, white paper, ebook or comparison guide page. Google clicks are counted by page, so campaigns don't have them. Job application forms are left out, the same as on the Leads tab.

Search Console counts Google clicks day by day here, which can come out up to about 1% above the page totals on the Google search tab.
