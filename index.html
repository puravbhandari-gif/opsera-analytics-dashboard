Robots: visits from people compared with all visits

Email software opens our emails and clicks every link to check them for safety. Each of those clicks shows up as a visit. Those robot visits almost never stay or click around, so they show up as visits that didn't engage.

Suggestion, not decided yet: count "people only" as engaged visits, Google's own rule. A visit counts as engaged if it lasted at least 10 seconds, looked at 2 or more pages, or filled in a form. This also leaves out some real people who left quickly. Compare the lines below and tell us which one to use.
