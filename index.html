Where visits came from

Each match

A visit can match twice, once by its page and once by its campaign. It then shows on both rows but counts once in the total.

More detail

Visits count people who arrived on the page from outside the site. A page people reach by clicking around shows few of them. Downloads are form fills on a report, white paper, ebook or comparison guide page. Search Console counts Google clicks by page, so campaigns don't have them. We leave out job application forms, the same as on the Leads tab.

Emails, lists and LinkedIn posts match whole words in their name or text, and show all dates, not just the period above. They're never added into the website numbers.

Search Console counts Google clicks day by day here, which can come out up to about 1% above the page totals on the Google search tab.
